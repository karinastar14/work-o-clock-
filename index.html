Karina is a design student. Work O’clock is her concept app, made for a design course in 2026: a project about giving the shape of a day to something that usually just disappears.

The concept

Make time visible.

The idea

A day is a clock.
A task is a ring.
Colour is a track.

Parallel lines (tracks) fold into rings, after Yusaku Kamekura: bold geometry, few colours, one idea per image. The tagline is “Your day, right now.” and the promise is “Make time visible.”

Typography

Two voices.

Heebo is the app’s typeface, because it reads well in Hebrew and Latin. Inter Black is the display face for posters, the teaser and the site headlines. Weights used: 500, 700, 800, 900. No italics, no underlines.

Layout and motion

One column. We climb.

ScreenOne phone-sized dark column. Add (+) top-left, Back bottom, actions inside thumb reach
NavigationEverything scrolls. Scroll is the climb, one stage after another
Easingcubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1), about 0.8s for reveals
RingCloses as time passes, then a celebration burst
Celebration wordsGood job. · You’re done. · Nailed it. · That’s a wrap. · Ring closed.
Reduced motionRespected everywhere, motion falls back to still
Voice

Short. Positive. Now.

Present tense, short sentences, a full stop at the end. Human and a little dry, never preachy.

Yes: Your day, right now. · Make time visible. · We climb. · Totally free day? Doubt it.

No: long explanations, exclamation marks, guilt.

DO
  • Keep the background black.
  • One colour = one track.
  • Big type tight, small labels wide.
  • Put the title on screen the moment the screen appears.
  • Keep everything inside thumb reach.
DON’T
  • Don’t use a blue background.
  • Don’t add arrowheads to tracks.
  • Don’t fill, outline or rotate the logo.
  • Don’t put more than one headline on a poster.
  • Don’t add a gradient.
